# Gap Analysis

| Existing Approach | What It Provides | Remaining Gap |
|---|---|---|
| eCourts / Case Status | Case status, hearings, orders and case information | Information is not presented as one complete, easy-to-understand case journey |
| Legal Research Platforms | Judgments and legal information | Users still need to interpret and connect the information |
| Legal Information Websites | Acts, rules and legal resources | Mostly information access rather than case-specific understanding |
| Lawyer-Client Communication | Case updates through the lawyer | Updates can remain dependent on individual communication |
| Separate Legal Tools | Individual research or document functions | Users may need multiple tools for different stages of a case |

## NyaySutra's Gap

NyaySutra focuses on connecting **case visibility, legal understanding, document intelligence, research assistance and case management** into one role-based platform.

The goal is not to replace existing judicial systems but to make the information around a case easier to understand, follow and manage.
