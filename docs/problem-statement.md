# Problem Statement

Legal cases involve court orders, notices, evidence, hearings and previous judgments, but this information is often difficult for citizens to understand and track.

Case updates can remain scattered across documents, hearings and lawyer communication, making it difficult for a person to clearly know what has happened, what is pending and what needs to happen next.

At the same time, lawyers spend significant time reviewing lengthy legal documents and searching for relevant previous judgments.

The gap is not simply the lack of legal information — it is the lack of a clear, connected and understandable view of that information.
