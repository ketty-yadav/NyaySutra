# Problem Statement

For many people, having a case number does not mean knowing what is actually happening in their case.

Legal information exists across court records, orders, documents, hearings, lawyers, and different online sources, but it is difficult to understand and follow as one connected case journey.

Citizens often struggle to understand what happened during a hearing, what a court order means, what action is required next, which documents are needed, and when an important deadline is approaching.

Legal documents and judgments are also written in complex legal language, creating an additional barrier for people without legal knowledge.

For lawyers, lengthy documents, large volumes of case information and the need to find relevant previous judgments can make legal research and preparation time-consuming.

The problem is therefore not simply that legal information is unavailable.

**The information exists — but it is scattered, difficult to understand, and not presented as one clear, continuous view of the case.**

This creates a gap between **having access to legal information** and **actually understanding and following a legal case.**
