# Key Features

## Case Management
Unique Case ID, case timeline, hearing history, documents and case updates in one place.

## Legal Document Summarizer
Converts lengthy judgments, notices, orders and legal documents into concise summaries containing the important information.

## Legal Language Simplifier
Explains complex legal terms, documents and court orders in simple English or Hindi.

## Legal Precedent Finder
Finds relevant previous judgments based on the facts and legal context of a case.

## Contract & Legal Notice Analyzer
Highlights important clauses, obligations, penalties, risks and other conditions in legal documents.

## Case Strength Assessment
Organizes supporting evidence, missing documents and comparatively strong or weak areas of a case.

## Court Delay Prediction
Estimates expected case duration using case characteristics, hearing history and available judicial trends.

## Hearing Preparation Assistant
Creates a preparation checklist containing documents, evidence, pending tasks and important hearing information.

## Property Dispute Assistant
Organizes property-related documents and ownership information into a structured timeline.

## Smart Reminder System
Provides reminders for hearings, deadlines, document submissions, payments and other important legal actions.

## Secure Digital Case Locker
Stores case documents and evidence with controlled access and secure storage.

## Voice Assistant
Allows users to ask questions about their case using simple voice-based queries.

## Multilingual Support
Provides legal information initially in English and Hindi, with scope for additional Indian languages.
