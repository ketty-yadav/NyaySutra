# Proposed Solution

NyaySutra brings the important parts of a legal case into one role-based platform.

It provides citizens with a clear view of their case status, hearings, documents, lawyer updates, deadlines and case timeline.

For legal professionals, it assists with document analysis, legal research, precedent discovery, hearing preparation and case information management.

The platform uses intelligent processing to simplify complex legal information while keeping final legal advice and decisions with qualified legal professionals.

NyaySutra is designed to make legal information more understandable, connected and accessible without replacing lawyers or judges.
